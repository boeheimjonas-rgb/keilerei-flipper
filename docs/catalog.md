A tiny dot-matrix beat-'em-up in the style of old LCD handhelds, made for the 128x64 screen.

## Gameplay
- Fight through **enemy waves**: Grunt, Runner, Brute, Shooter and a **Boss** every 5th wave.
- Smash **crates** to find weapons: Club (knockback), Sword (3-cell arc), Axe (range 2), Pistol (12 rounds), Shield (blocks 5 hits) and Hearts. Weapons wear out.
- The **arena changes** every wave: Factory Hall, Forest, Conveyor Belts, Kiln with pulsing lava, Ice Storage and Night Shift with a light cone only.
- After wave 10 the cycle repeats harder. High score is saved.

## Controls
- **D-pad**: move
- **OK**: attack / shoot (hold for auto attack)
- **Back**: pause (on title screen: exit)
- **Hold Back**: exit
